Ukrainian

Українська

Ukrainian has four letters Russian does not: і, ї, є and ґ. Its vocabulary is in places closer to Polish than to Russian, a legacy of centuries under Polish-Lithuanian administration. Under the Russian Empire its printing was banned more than once in the nineteenth century, the language being officially held to be a dialect. It is today the second Slavic language by number of speakers.

How to spot it

Four letters Russian does not have: і, ї, є, ґ. Plus a frequent apostrophe (п’ю).

A few sentences

  • Я дуже люблю гуляти парком восени.

    I really love walking in the park in autumn.

  • Я купив свіжий хліб у пекарні.

    I bought fresh bread at the bakery.

  • У мене зламався телефон.

    My phone is broken.

  • Я купив квитки на концерт.

    I bought tickets for the concert.

  • Він працює водієм автобуса.

    He works as a bus driver.
