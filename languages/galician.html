Galician

Galego

Galician and Portuguese were a single language in the Middle Ages, that of the Galician-Portuguese lyric. Political separation drove them apart, but the kinship is still obvious: Galician looks like Portuguese written with Spanish conventions. The nasal sound is gone — non where Portuguese writes não — and the spelling uses x where Spanish uses j.

How to spot it

A few sentences

  • Esta mañá fomos ao mercado mercar peixe e pan.

    This morning we went to the market to buy fish and bread.

  • Esta noite imos cear fóra.

    We are eating out tonight.

  • Teño que ir ao banco esta tarde.

    I have to go to the bank this afternoon.

  • O coche fai un ruído raro.

    The car is making a strange noise.

  • A miña veciña ten tres gatos.

    My neighbour has three cats.
