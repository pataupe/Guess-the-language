Estonian

Eesti

Estonian is Finnish's close cousin, though that does not make them mutually intelligible. It lost the final vowels Finnish keeps, which makes its words distinctly shorter. Its letter õ does not exist in Finnish. It distinguishes three degrees of length for vowels and consonants, a refinement rare among the world's languages.

How to spot it

A cousin of Finnish, but with the letter õ and shorter words — the final vowels have dropped away.

A few sentences

  • Ma sõidan igal hommikul rongiga tööle.

    Every morning I take the train to work.

  • Suvel käime mere ääres puhkamas.

    In summer we go to the seaside to rest.

  • Unustasin prillid kontorisse.

    I left my glasses at the office.

  • Supp on liiga kuum.

    The soup is too hot.

  • Raamatukogu on täna kinni.

    The library is closed today.
