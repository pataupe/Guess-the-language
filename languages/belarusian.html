Belarusian

Беларуская

Belarusian is the only language to write the letter ў, a short u found in no other Cyrillic alphabet. Its spelling is frankly phonetic: you write what you hear, including unstressed o reduced to a, where Russian keeps the original letter. Its everyday use has receded sharply in favour of Russian, which dominates public life in the country.

How to spot it

The only Cyrillic language with the letter ў (short u). Plenty of а where Russian writes о.

A few sentences

  • Кожную раніцу я п’ю кубак гарбаты перад працай.

    Every morning I drink a cup of tea before work.

  • Зімой тут вельмі холадна.

    It is very cold here in winter.

  • Мне трэба памыць посуд.

    I need to do the washing-up.

  • Малыш заснуў на руках.

    The little one fell asleep in my arms.

  • Я не ведаю, дзе мы паставілі машыну.

    I do not know where we left the car.
