Irish

Gaeilge

Irish is the first official language of Ireland, but only a minority speak it daily, chiefly in the Gaeltacht regions. It puts the verb first in the sentence, an order rare in Europe, and uses initial mutations: the beginning of a word changes according to its grammatical context, which makes the dictionary hard to use for a beginner. Its written literature is the oldest in western Europe after Latin and Greek.

How to spot it

Acute accents only (á é í ó ú) and the clusters bh, mh, dh, gh everywhere. The verb comes first in the sentence.

A few sentences

  • Ólaim tae gach maidin.

    I drink tea every morning.

  • Is maith liom ceol traidisiúnta.

    I like traditional music.

  • Tá sé ag cur sneachta.

    It is snowing.

  • Tá an carr briste.

    The car is broken.

  • Is maith liom tae láidir.

    I like strong tea.
