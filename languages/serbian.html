Serbian

Српски

Serbian has a rare feature: it is officially written in two alphabets, Cyrillic and Latin, and its speakers switch between them without thinking. Its Cyrillic was reformed in the nineteenth century by Vuk Karadžić on a strict principle — one letter per sound, one sound per letter — which makes it one of the most regular spelling systems in Europe.

How to spot it

South Slavic Cyrillic with ј, љ, њ, ћ, ђ. No ы or ё as in Russian. (It is also written in the Latin alphabet.)

A few sentences

  • Сваког јутра трчим у парку.

    Every morning I go running in the park.

  • Мој деда је био рибар.

    My grandfather was a fisherman.

  • Изгубио сам наочаре.

    I have lost my glasses.

  • Сутра идем на пијацу.

    Tomorrow I am going to the market.

  • Моја ћерка слика веома лепо.

    My daughter draws very well.
