Picard

Picard

Picard is a Romance language of northern France and neighbouring Wallonia. Close to French without being a patois of it, it descends straight from Latin, just as French does. Ch'ti, its northern variety, became the best known after the success of a 2008 film. It keeps the k sound where French says “ch” (cat for chat) and pronounces “ch” where French says “s” or soft “c” (chinq for cinq).

How to spot it

“ch’” for the, this (ch’est, ch’matin), “eune” for a, “min / tin / sin” for my / your / his, “tchien” for dog, “agache” for look and “j’allons” for we go.

A few sentences

  • Min tchien i dort din sin panier.

    My dog is sleeping in its basket.

  • Les ch’tiots i mingent eune gaufre din ch’jardin.

    The little ones are eating a waffle in the garden.

  • Eune bonne tarte aux pommes, ch’est tout ch’qu’i m’faut.

    A good apple tart is all I need.

  • Qué qu’t’as à m’raconter ?

    What have you got to tell me?

  • Ch’est tin tour d’faire ch’vaisselle.

    It is your turn to do the dishes.
