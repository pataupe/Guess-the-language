Polish

Polski

Polish is the most spoken West Slavic language and the only major Slavic language written in a Latin alphabet extended with ł, ą and ę. It keeps seven cases and a well-earned reputation for consonant clusters, which the digraphs sz, cz, rz and szcz make more daunting to the eye than to the ear. Its written standard has been stable since the sixteenth century.

How to spot it

The barred ł, the nasals ą and ę, and the clusters sz, cz, rz, szcz. Very recognisable.

A few sentences

  • Wczoraj wieczorem oglądaliśmy razem bardzo dobry film.

    Last night we watched a very good film together.

  • Latem jeździmy w góry z całą rodziną.

    In summer we go to the mountains with the whole family.

  • Zgubiłem portfel w autobusie.

    I lost my wallet on the bus.

  • Woda w kranie jest zimna.

    The tap water is cold.

  • Dziadek naprawia stary zegar.

    Grandpa is mending an old clock.
