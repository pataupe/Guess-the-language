Swedish

Svenska

Swedish is the most spoken of the Scandinavian languages and is official in Finland as well as Sweden. Its definite article is a suffix: hus means house, huset the house. It has a pitch accent that distinguishes otherwise identical words, a rare feature in Europe. Swedish, Norwegian and Danish remain largely mutually intelligible in writing.

How to spot it

Three letters of its own: å, ä, ö. The definite article is glued to the end of the word (huset = the house).

A few sentences

  • Jag dricker alltid en kopp kaffe på morgonen.

    I always drink a cup of coffee in the morning.

  • Min familj bor långt uppe i norr.

    My family lives far up north.

  • Jag har glömt min telefon på kontoret.

    I left my phone at the office.

  • Han arbetar på ett kontor i centrum.

    He works in an office in the centre.

  • Vi målade om köket förra veckan.

    We repainted the kitchen last week.
