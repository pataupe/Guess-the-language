Spanish

Español

Spanish has the second-largest number of native speakers in the world, far beyond its original European population. Its spelling is among the most regular in Europe: you pronounce very nearly what is written. It is the only language to open questions and exclamations with an inverted mark, ¿ and ¡. The ñ, born from a medieval shorthand for a double n, has become its emblem.

How to spot it

The ñ, the upside-down ¿ and ¡, the doubles ll and rr, and a great many words ending in -ción.

A few sentences

  • Todos los domingos comemos en casa de mi abuela.

    Every Sunday we eat at my grandmother’s house.

  • Mi hermano estudia arquitectura en la universidad.

    My brother studies architecture at university.

  • Me he levantado con dolor de cabeza.

    I woke up with a headache.

  • El perro se ha escapado otra vez.

    The dog has escaped again.

  • Me duele la espalda de tanto estar sentado.

    My back aches from sitting so much.
