Catalan

Català

Catalan is not a dialect of Spanish but a separate Romance language, in some respects closer to Occitan and French than to Castilian. It is spoken in Catalonia, Valencia, the Balearics and Andorra, where it is the sole official language. Its most distinctive mark is the raised dot of l·l, which separates two l sounds pronounced apart.

How to spot it

The raised dot of l·l, apostrophes everywhere (l’home, d’aquí) and the word “amb”. Sits between Spanish and French.

A few sentences

  • Demà anirem a la muntanya si fa bon temps.

    Tomorrow we will go to the mountains if the weather is good.

  • Aquest any l’estiu ha estat molt calorós.

    The summer has been very hot this year.

  • He perdut les claus de casa.

    I have lost the house keys.

  • El meu fill ha començat a caminar.

    My son has started walking.

  • El pa d’ahir ja està dur.

    Yesterday’s bread has already gone hard.
