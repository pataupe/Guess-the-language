Friulian

Furlan

Friulian is a distinct Romance language, officially recognised, often grouped with Ladin and Romansh under the label Rhaeto-Romance. Like French it keeps the final s of plurals, and it palatalises Latin c and g before a, giving cjase (house) or gjat (cat). It has had an official spelling since 1996, though several local varieties still stand apart.

How to spot it

The groups “cj” and “gj” (cjase, cjan, gjat), circumflex accents (frêt, amîs, sûr) and a small “o” placed before the verb (o ai, o soi).

A few sentences

  • O voi a cjase, o soi stanc.

    I am going home, I am tired.

  • Cheste gnot al à nevât, il mont al è dut blanc.

    It snowed last night, everything is white.

  • Ce ore ise? O soi in ritart!

    What time is it? I am late!

  • Cjale, e rive la corriere!

    Look, the coach is coming!

  • Il zornâl al è sul taule.

    The newspaper is on the table.
