Occitan

Occitan, or the langue d'oc, is a Romance language spoken in southern France, a few valleys of Italy and the Val d'Aran. It covers several major dialects, Languedocian, Provençal, Gascon, Limousin, Auvergnat and Vivaro-Alpine, which the classical spelling tries to bring together. In the Middle Ages the troubadours made it one of the first great literary languages of Romance Europe.

How to spot it

Classical spelling: lh and nh (filha, ensenhar), the grave ò (fòrça, pichòt), the articles lo / la / los / las and the negation “pas” placed after the verb.

A few sentences

  • Uèi fa fòrça caud, demòra a l’ombra.

    It is very hot today, stay in the shade.

  • Aquel libre es tròp car, lo vòli pas crompar.

    This book is too expensive, I don’t want to buy it.

  • Fa polit, anem nos passejar.

    The weather is lovely, let’s go for a walk.

  • La nòstra vesina cultiva de tomatas dins son òrt.

    Our neighbour grows tomatoes in her vegetable garden.

  • Podètz me dire ont es la gara?

    Can you tell me where the station is?
