Romanian

Română

Romanian is the only Romance language of eastern Europe, cut off from its sisters by a belt of Slavic languages. That isolation explains its vocabulary, Latin at the core but heavily borrowed from Slavic, Hungarian and Turkish. It kept grammatical cases the other Romance languages lost, and its definite article attaches to the end of the word, as in the neighbouring Balkan languages.

How to spot it

A Romance language with comma-below letters: ș, ț, plus ă and î. The article is glued to the end (trenul = the train).

A few sentences

  • Vremea este foarte frumoasă astăzi, hai să ieșim.

    The weather is beautiful today, let’s go out.

  • Bunicii mei au o casă la țară.

    My grandparents have a house in the countryside.

  • Am uitat să închid gazul.

    I forgot to turn off the gas.

  • Mi-am pierdut cheile de la birou.

    I have lost the office keys.

  • Televizorul nu mai merge.

    The television has stopped working.
