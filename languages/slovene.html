Slovene

Slovenščina

Slovene has kept the dual, a grammatical form reserved for exactly two people or things, which almost every other Indo-European language has abandoned. For such a small territory it is remarkably fragmented: dozens of dialects are counted, some barely intelligible to one another. Its alphabet adds only three special signs, č, š and ž.

How to spot it

Only three accents: č, š, ž. It keeps the dual (a special form for exactly two) and uses “je” for “is”.

A few sentences

  • Vsako jutro grem v službo s kolesom.

    Every morning I cycle to work.

  • Poleti se radi kopamo v jezeru.

    In summer we love swimming in the lake.

  • Pozabil sem kupiti mleko.

    I forgot to buy milk.

  • Moram oprati avto.

    I need to wash the car.

  • Moj brat igra v bandu.

    My brother plays in a band.
