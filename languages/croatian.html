Croatian

Hrvatski

Croatian belongs to a continuum it shares with Serbian, Bosnian and Montenegrin: spoken, their speakers understand one another without difficulty. What separates them is as much historical and political as linguistic. Croatian is written only in the Latin alphabet, with č, ć, š, ž and đ, and tends to prefer words built on Slavic roots where Serbian more readily accepts loanwords.

How to spot it

č, ć, š, ž, đ in the Latin alphabet. Key words: “je”, “ne”, “gdje”. Very close to Serbian and Bosnian.

A few sentences

  • Ljeti najviše volim jedriti.

    In summer what I love most is sailing.

  • Ova kava je prehladna.

    This coffee is too cold.

  • Pokvario mi se hladnjak.

    My fridge has broken down.

  • Moram oprati prozore.

    I need to clean the windows.

  • Moj sin svira bubnjeve.

    My son plays the drums.
