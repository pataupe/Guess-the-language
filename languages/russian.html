Russian

Русский

Russian is the most spoken Slavic language and served as the lingua franca across the Soviet sphere. It has no articles but six cases, and above all a system of verbal aspect — every verb exists in two versions depending on whether the action is seen as completed — which remains one of the main obstacles for learners. Its alphabet descends from the Cyrillic devised in the ninth century on a Greek model.

How to spot it

Cyrillic with ы, э, ъ and ё. Words ending in -ый, -ого and the particle “не” are typically Russian.

A few sentences

  • Я нигде не могу найти ключи от машины.

    I cannot find the car keys anywhere.

  • Извините, вы не подскажете, где здесь аптека?

    Excuse me, could you tell me where the pharmacy is?

  • Я поставил чайник на плиту.

    I put the kettle on.

  • У нас закончился хлеб.

    We have run out of bread.

  • Я не помню его имени.

    I cannot remember his name.
