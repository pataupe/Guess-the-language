Bulgarian

Български

Bulgarian is the grammatical exception of the Slavic world: it lost the case endings all its relatives kept, and gained in exchange a definite article attached to the end of the word. Both traits align it with its Balkan neighbours rather than with its own family. It is also a Bulgarian dialect that gave rise to Old Church Slavonic, the first Slavic language ever written down.

How to spot it

Cyrillic with a very frequent ъ in the middle of words. No cases, but the article is glued to the end (градът).

A few sentences

  • Утре ще ходим на море с цялото семейство.

    Tomorrow we are going to the seaside with the whole family.

  • Купих пресни домати от пазара.

    I bought fresh tomatoes at the market.

  • Забравих да изключа печката.

    I forgot to turn off the cooker.

  • Трябва да платя сметките.

    I have to pay the bills.

  • Влакът тръгва след малко.

    The train leaves in a moment.
