Lithuanian

Lietuvių

Lithuanian is famous for its archaism: it has kept features of Proto-Indo-European that almost every other language in the family has lost, which makes it a cornerstone of comparative linguistics. Together with Latvian it is all that survives of the Baltic branch, every other Baltic language having died out. Its stress is mobile and unpredictable, which makes it considerably harder to learn.

How to spot it

Hooked vowels ą ę į ų, plus ū and ė. Words very often end in -as, -is, -us.

A few sentences

  • Kiekvieną rytą einu pėsčiomis į darbą.

    Every morning I walk to work.

  • Vasarą dažnai važiuojame prie ežero.

    In summer we often drive out to the lake.

  • Pamiršau paimti skėtį.

    I forgot to take the umbrella.

  • Mano brolis mokosi vairuoti.

    My brother is learning to drive.

  • Katė pabėgo pro langą.

    The cat escaped through the window.
