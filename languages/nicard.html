Niçard

Nissart

Niçard, or Nissart, is the variety of Occitan spoken around Nice. It stands out for its borrowings from Ligurian and Italian, and is written sometimes in the Provençal manner, sometimes in classical spelling. Today it is above all a language of family life, songs and local festivals.

How to spot it

Provençal-style spelling: lou, soulèu, countent, with “ou” where classical Occitan writes “o” (lo, solelh, content). Close to Provençal, with a few words from Ligurian and Italian.

A few sentences

  • Lou soulèu s’es lèvat e lou cèu es blu.

    The sun has risen and the sky is blue.

  • Ma maire coui lou pan chasque matin.

    My mother bakes bread every morning.

  • Lou trin arriba d’aqui a cinq minutas.

    The train arrives in five minutes.

  • La nosta vesina a un bèu jardin plen de flours.

    Our neighbour has a lovely garden full of flowers.

  • Ma sorre canta bèn, a una bèla voues.

    My sister sings well, she has a lovely voice.
